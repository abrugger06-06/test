# Judge brief: Fluxcase static Instagram ads (set of 34)

You are an independent judge. Score the SET out of 10, and give every ad its own score out of 10.
Be honest: do not inflate and do not invent problems. A 10 means you would run that ad unchanged in a
paid Instagram campaign and can name nothing concrete that would make it better. The set scores 10
only if every ad scores 10. Every point off needs a specific, fixable reason and the exact fix.

## What they are
34 static ads for Fluxcase (30, plus 31-34: two film frames the owner picked, each as 4:5 and 1:1), rendered from the real CAD (no stock, no AI images), announcing the
Kickstarter launch on November 1, 2026. Formats: 4:5 feed (1080x1350), 1:1 (1080x1080) and 9:16 story
(1080x1920; keep copy out of the top 250 px and bottom 330 px that Instagram's UI covers).
The owner asked for them to be short and simple, and sent a reference picture they love
(`owner-reference.png` in the round folder: the launch video's end card, an older version that still
had the maker line, which has since been removed). Judge each ad against that direction: FLUXCASE
wordmark with a deep-teal rule, a heavy headline with one deep-teal accent, a second line in Archivo
800, black cases large on a light ground, lineups that run off the edge.

## Rules
- No invented claims: no backer counts, funding goals, reviews, ratings, dimensions, weights, prices,
  strength claims, or anything not in the allowed facts. No vague slogans: say plainly what it is.
- No em dashes. No purple gradients, no pill-shaped buttons, no emoji as icons.
- Cases are black PETG only. Magnets are always silver.
- The maker's name (The 3D Print Depot) must not appear anywhere.
- Wherever vials are shown, a small "Vials not included." line must be on the ad.
- Brand: ink #111413, background #F3F4F2, render grey #E2E2E2, teal #74CABF, deep teal #1D6B62.
  Type: Archivo (wide, heavy) for headlines, JetBrains Mono for small text.
- Copy must be legible on a phone and must not collide with the product or other text.

## Allowed facts
Magnets (solid discs, never rings) in the base and lid pull the case shut and hold it there; no clips, no friction-fit lid.
PETG, 3D printed to order. (Do not mention Texas: owner removed it.) Five cases: 12-Vial 3 mL, 6-Vial 3 mL, 4-Vial 10 mL, 8-Vial 10 mL,
7-Vial Mixed (holds 3 mL and 10 mL vials). For 3 mL and 10 mL vials. Kickstarter launch November 1, 2026.

## What to review (in the round folder you are given)
- `ads-sheet-1.jpg` .. `ads-sheet-4.jpg`: all 34 at a glance, numbered.
- `ad-01.png` .. `ad-34.png`: each ad at full resolution. Open every one.
