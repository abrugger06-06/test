# Grading brief: Fluxcase Kickstarter promo v3 (lifestyle cut)

The owner rejected the previous cut (the one you passed in round 9 of PROMO-BRIEF) with: "I don't like
this direction." Asked what they wanted, they chose **Lifestyle / in-use** ("show the case in real use:
on a desk, a kitchen counter, a nightstand, in a travel bag; warmer, real-world feel instead of a studio
product demo") and said the old cut was **too slow / boring**. This is a new film built for that brief.

Judge it as a paid Kickstarter ad in that lifestyle, high-energy direction. Everything else in
PROMO-BRIEF.md still applies: the score format, the hard rules (no invented claims, no em dashes, black
PETG only, solid silver disc magnets, no maker name or Texas, "Vials not included." whenever vials are
visible, 0.3 s per word, hook in 2 s, cuts on beats, brand colours and type), and the owner decisions
(CTA "Follow it on Kickstarter.", "Renders shown." on the end card, all five cases on the end card, an
empty case for the tip-over). Read PROMO-BRIEF.md for those.

## The film
21 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, rendered from the real CAD (three.js) on lifestyle sets built in code: a sunlit
oak desk (coffee, notebook, laptop, plant), a quartz kitchen counter with subway tile, a walnut nightstand
with a lamp, a linen travel set (towel, passport, pouch), a pale oak shelf. Window-light shadows, depth of
field, a light grain and vignette. Soundtrack: scripts/music3.py (120 BPM, kit in from frame 0).
Renderer: site/life.html, sets in site/sets.js. Storyboard: ../brag-plan.md.

Review files are in the round folder named in your message (sheets every 0.25 s, key frames, frames/,
waveform.png, music.wav), as before.
