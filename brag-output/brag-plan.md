# Fluxcase Kickstarter promo (v3, lifestyle cut): storyboard

19.5 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps. The owner turned down the studio cut ("I don't like this direction") and asked for
**lifestyle / in-use** with **more energy**. Built from the real CAD (`FluxCase_all_cases_silver_magnets.3mf`)
on lifestyle sets made in code: sunlit oak desk, quartz kitchen counter, walnut nightstand, linen travel set,
pale oak shelf. Window-light shadows, depth of field, light grain. Renderer `work/site/life.html` (sets in
`work/site/sets.js`); music `work/scripts/music3.py`, 120 BPM, D major, Rhodes keys, claps, shaker and set foley, full hit on frame 0. Cuts on beats:
2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 6.5, 9.5, 12.0, 14.5, 16.0; ends 19.5. The studio cut is kept in
`work/judge/brag-plan-v2.md` and `work/site/promo.html`.

Decisions carried over: black PETG only, solid silver disc magnets, no dimensions, prices or invented
claims, no maker name or Texas, "Vials not included." whenever vials are on screen, empty case for the
tip-over, CTA "Follow it on Kickstarter." with "Renders shown.".
